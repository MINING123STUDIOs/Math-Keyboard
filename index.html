Twelve small, self-contained browser tools. No frameworks, no dependencies, no tracking — just open and use.

Math & Computation

Math Keyboard

A virtual keyboard of ~3,700 math symbols with search, recent-history, copy and download.

1-Bit Calculator

Compute AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR and NOT on single bits A and B.

Numerical Analysis

Arbitrary-precision math in your browser: big-number calculator, calculus and more, up to 100,000 digits.

Launch

Language & Meaning

💬

Colorless Green Ideas

A Chomsky-style sentence generator. Perfect syntax, meaningless semantics, numbers up to a googol.

Launch
📚

Library of Babel

Type any string and find the exact book whose first line is your words. 95 glyphs, 262144 pages per book, ~5.1×101,659,034,805 volumes.

Launch

Machines & Security

✍️

Clean Editor

A fast, minimal code editor with syntax styling, light/dark theme and keyboard-friendly controls.

Launch
⚙️

TM0 Visualizer

A toolchain for TM0, a tiny Turing-machine dialect: write assembly or high-level IR, compile to .tm0 and watch it run.

Launch
🔐

Secure Channel Assistant

An end-to-end encrypted messaging demo with a Signal-style Double Ratchet key exchange, running entirely in your browser.

Launch

Cellular Automata

🪨

Cell Battles

Two cell types — ROCK and PAPER — fight it out in a probabilistic cellular automaton with 12 themes.

Launch
🌱

Elementary CA Explorer

All 256 elementary cellular automaton rules, from Rule 30 chaos to Rule 110 Turing-completeness.

Launch

Worlds & Physics

💧

Liquid Lab

An interactive Navier-Stokes flow field with views for direction, speed, curl, pressure, and heat. Drag to stir, pour heat from the nozzle.

Launch
🗺️

Landscape Explorer

An infinite procedural world — perfect mazes, Hilbert curves, caves, biomes and more, computed in a Web Worker. Pan, zoom, play, dodge hunters, slide on ice.

About
